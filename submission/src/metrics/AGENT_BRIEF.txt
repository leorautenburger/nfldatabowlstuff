DATA FACTS (important)
- Coordinates are standardized: offense always moves toward +x. x_rel = yards past line of scrimmage
  (negative = backfield). y in [0, 53.3]. vx = downfield velocity, vy = lateral velocity.
  t = seconds since snap (t=0 is the snap frame). 10 frames/sec.
- Tracking ends ~0.5 s after the throw (or at sack/scramble). There is NO tracking of the ball flight
  or catch point. "Throw frame" (plays.throw_frame / routes *_end columns) is the last reliable look.
- No targeted-receiver column exists; plays.targetNflId was parsed from playDescription (7,310 of
  7,565 pass attempts matched). player_plays.is_target / is_catch derive from it.
- Every play is a dropback (no designed runs). plays.pressure = any PFF hit/hurry/sack on the play.
- plays: context columns from plays.csv + week, split, is_train, snap_frame, throw_frame, end_frame,
  end_event (throw/other_stop), los_x, ball_y, time_to_throw, time_to_end, yards_to_goal, red_zone,
  goal_to_go, targetNflId, pressure, n_rushers, n_pass_blockers, n_routes, n_te_on_field.
- player_plays: PFF scouting x players + x_rel_snap, y_snap, lat_from_ball, alignment
  (inline / wing / detached / backfield), is_target, is_catch, split, is_train, displayName, officialPosition.
- te_plays: player_plays restricted to officialPosition == "TE" (10,496 rows, 121 TEs) +
  assignment in {route, chip_release (Pass Route with blockType CH or SR), pass_block},
  release_t, release_frame, max_depth, x_rel_end, sep_end, receiver_num, n_rec_side.
- routes: one row per route runner per play, ALL positions (39,398): x_rel_snap, y_snap, sep_snap,
  nd_dx_snap/nd_dy_snap (nearest defender offset at snap), *_end = at end of dropback (throw frame):
  t_end, x_rel_end, y_end, sep_end, n_def_3_end, n_def_5_end, lane_end, s_end, vx_end, vy_end,
  max_depth, min_sep, max_sep, release_t/release_frame (NaN = never released), alignment,
  officialPosition, pff_positionLinedUp, pff_blockType, receiver_num (1 = widest on that side),
  n_rec_side, side, abs_lat, is_target, is_catch, time_to_throw, end_event.
- receiver_frames: per frame per route runner, snap -> end of dropback (1.26M rows): t, x, y, x_rel,
  s, a, vx, vy, dir, o, qb_x, qb_y, sep (nearest defender), sep2 (2nd nearest), n_def_3, n_def_5,
  lane_clear (min defender distance to QB->receiver segment, ignoring defenders at the QB end),
  nearest_def_id, nd_x, nd_y, dist_qb, released (bool, cumulative), split, is_train.
- tracking (7.3M rows, all 22 players + ball, snap -> last frame): gameId, playId, nflId, frameId, t,
  team, is_off, is_def, pff_role, officialPosition, x, y, x_rel, s, a, dis, o, dir, vx, vy, event.
- Release definition (shared, in config): first frame where displacement from snap position
  (downfield or lateral) >= 1.5 yd AND speed >= 2.5 yd/s held for 3 frames.
