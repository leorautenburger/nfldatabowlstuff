METHODS

What every score means

TE Versatility Scout separates what a tight end is asked to do from how well they perform. The player profile shows performance percentiles; Deployment Breadth and role shares explain usage without increasing a player’s performance score.

Reading the profile

Percentiles

Each axis is converted to a 0–100 percentile among TEs with that component available. Higher is better after score direction is aligned; Route Release Tax is inverted because a lower delay is better.

Overall score

The overall score is the unweighted mean of the five fully covered pass-game axes. It intentionally excludes Deployment Breadth and the provisional Run-Game Impact axis.

Samples and shrinkage

Component outputs retain their opportunity counts. Where supplied, empirical-Bayes shrunk estimates are used so small samples move toward the league mean rather than producing extreme player rankings.

Performance axes

AXIS 1

Protection Impact

60% Protection Value Added + 40% Edge Seal Sustainability

Protection Value Added (PVA) estimates the change in expected pressure if TE blocks and chips are removed from the protection structure. Edge Seal Sustainability measures whether the TE sustains protection against the assigned edge threat.

AXIS 2

Chip-to-Route Value

40% PAV + 40% CSR + 20% inverted Release Tax

Protection-to-Availability Value (PAV) is extra protection-window time after a chip, multiplied by the TE’s receiving availability. Chip-to-Separation Return (CSR) measures separation retained after the chip. Route Release Tax measures the cost of delayed release.

AXIS 3

Route Threat

34% Eligible Threat Rate + 33% Separation Over Expected + 33% Middle-of-Field Access

Eligible Threat Rate measures timely viable receiving options. Separation Over Expected compares observed separation with route/context expectation. Middle-of-Field Access measures viable central throwing windows at fixed dropback times.

AXIS 4

Coverage Stress

50% Coverage Gravity + 25% Constraint Value + 25% Red-Zone Conflict

Coverage Gravity captures added defensive attention. Constraint Value measures defensive alignment changes when the TE aligns inline versus detached. Red-Zone Conflict measures route, run-fit, and protection ambiguity near the goal line.

AXIS 5

Open-Field Creation

100% YAC Runway

YAC Runway estimates post-catch open-field opportunity from defender spacing, momentum, and catch location. The profile uses the player-specific shrunk YAC-over-expected estimate.

AXIS 6 · PROVISIONAL

Run-Game Impact †

40% rush EPA on/off + 25% rush success on/off + 20% edge-run EPA + 15% short-yardage conversion

Run tilt, heavy-package usage, and box count are context fields, not score credit. The uploaded source table has no upstream methodology in this repository; this axis is visible and searchable but excluded from the overall score and performance-similarity model.

Deployment is context, not performance

Deployment Breadth is the context-adjusted Dual-Threat Deployment Index. It uses normalized entropy across inline route, detached route, chip-and-release, standard pass block, and stay-in/non-conventional pass block roles. Inverse-propensity weighting accounts for formation and personnel context.

Deployment Breadth drives role filters, archetypes, and role-similarity comparisons. It does not increase the overall performance score.

Scope and limitations

The tracking study covers 2021 Weeks 1–8 and is pass-play focused. Metrics are transparent, observational estimates—not proprietary NFL grades or causal claims. Interpret player percentiles alongside opportunity counts, confidence intervals, and the underlying play rows.
